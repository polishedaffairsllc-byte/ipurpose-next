## B. Edits before editable external deck/concept sheet
1. **Missing eight-slide storyboard in v2 PDF.** The 12 pages have copy, brief, session examples and design specs but no revised slide-by-slide eight-slide section or actual deck. The PDF itself says the next step is a deck. Reuse the v1 eight-slide outline, apply v2 edits and produce **actual editable slides**. Do not label v2 itself an editable deck or finished one-page designed sheet.
2. **UG one-page summary content reconciliation.** The one-page summary fields (p.10) omit several substantive elements from the original UG charter: *1–2 possible career/learning pathways, one verified source, why the choice was made, foreseeable obstacle, and optional AI verification/disclosure*. Keep one-page format, but add compact fields for **options and verified source; reason for chosen direction; first action/date and support; obstacle/response; optional responsible AI note**. Then compare line-by-line with `docs/ug-pilot-charter-discussion-draft-20261117.md`. Two-page private working sheets can hold further detail.
3. **Privacy copy is still absolute in the worksheets.** p.7: "Reflection pages are the participant's own confidential work and are not collected by iPurpose"; p.9: "This page is yours. iPurpose does not collect it." Accurate **only for a specifically implemented offline workbook workflow**; otherwise the hosted Clarity/Compass implementation and institution-controlled uploads may process records. Use "For this printable workbook activity, keep this page with you; do not submit it to iPurpose. You may choose what to discuss. Any institution/hosted data collection is governed by a separate approved notice." Do not assert broad confidentiality without legal/privacy review.
4. **90-minute session assumptions.** W1/W2 90-minute schedules (pp.7–8) imply 12 hours of facilitator contact time over 8 weeks if carried throughout. Is that within the cohort-license economics? Before quoting or finalizing the program, supply a standard **60-minute core agenda** plus optional **90-minute extended format**, or label 90-minute facilitation a specific selectable variant and cost it. Clarify session modality, staff prep and support hours.
5. **Pedagogy and sharing.** "Participants can say" the decision/values should mean **can record/identify privately**, not mandatory group disclosure; one Week 2 exercise asks three possibilities but "up to three" is more inclusive. Optional pair-share can discuss process, not private content; provide accessible written alternative.
6. **Source footnotes.** The price-free concept (p.5) cites "Strada, October 2026; NACE, October 2026" but no study titles, dates or accessible footnote links. Add exact study citations in buyer deck footnotes/speaker notes and sell-sheet bottom citation line; keep scope qualifiers. This is cited evidence about gaps, NOT efficacy or buyer demand.
7. **Draft approval language.** p.3 says "Research sentence approved for use [DRAFT]" despite outstanding founder approval; replace with "Internally verified for factual accuracy; pending founder approval and final source placement." A quote from a public report does not authorize claim "iPurpose works".

Retain Italiana display, Marcellus heading, proposed Source Sans 3 long-body only after founder grants the third-font exception; repo brand colors are checked but still test accessible final artifacts.
