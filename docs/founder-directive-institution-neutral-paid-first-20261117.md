# Founder Directive — Institution-Neutral, Paid-First iPurpose Licensing
**Effective:** 2026-11-17 • **Decision authority:** Founder & CEO • **Status:** Internal operating direction

## Business model
iPurpose is building **one licensable, facilitator-delivered, institution-neutral** Soul → Systems → AI™ curriculum and accompanying materials. Target buyers include universities/colleges, adult workforce-development providers, and entrepreneurship/cohort-training organizations. The institution provides participants, instruction, local supports and existing delivery infrastructure. iPurpose provides licensed authored materials, optional digital tools, onboarding and bounded support as separately contracted.

**No buyer or single university controls the roadmap before a paid, authorized engagement.** Product materials must remain neutral as to institution, country, academic program, participant referral service and grant funding. Customize only after a clearly scoped business case and founder approval. The curriculum should support participant agency through personal reflection, evidence-based options, decision, an achievable next step and an appropriate support pathway. A specific 14-day action window can be a suggested example, not a fixed universal requirement.

## Commercial policy
**New institutional pilots, workshops, licenses, integrations and customization are PAID by default.** Founder labor, IP development, curriculum design, facilitator prep, support, travel, technical adaptation, and compliance work all have real costs. No new no-fee offers or speculative buyer-specific development. If an organization wants a trial, sell a time- and scope-bounded paid pilot or short workshop. Proposed *internal, unvalidated* USD pricing:
- One eight-week institution-delivered cohort license (up to 40 adults): **$3,500**.
- An eight-week iPurpose-supported pilot: **$7,500**, with explicitly capped support/facilitator hours.
- An annual single-site license (up to 250 adults): **$12,000/year**.
- A 90-minute institutional workshop (up to 40): **$1,500**.
Every external quote requires delivery-cost verification, clarified inclusions/exclusions, contracting and founder approval. No assumption of available public/grant money or international purchasing power.

## University of Ghana (UGCCD): existing exception, NOT anchor
The University of Ghana's response remains **preliminary email interest only**; no signed agreement, authorization, confirmed sponsor, purchased license, scheduled pilot, or buyer commitment. It is one lead in an expanding multi-market pipeline, **not the anchor partner or main product specification**.

Honor the **previously communicated fee-waived concept** for one initial eight-week pilot up to 40 voluntary adult students, without automatically extending free work, spending, bespoke features, unlimited support, travel, or ongoing delivery. If talks advance, document mutual feasibility, who supplies facilitation and costs, written approvals, privacy/student safeguards and a fixed cap on iPurpose obligations. Founder may decline a proposed implementation if its actual unreimbursed costs or workload are not sustainable, while explaining scope faithfully and professionally.

**Do not change or interrupt scheduled university outreach.** No separate UG-specific worksheets, contact fields, academic claims or localization are required for the base licensing prototype; optional institution-specific adaptations are only considered when a genuine partner progresses.
