# iPurpose × University of Ghana | Draft Pilot Charter
**DISCUSSION DRAFT — NOT APPROVED BY THE UNIVERSITY OR THE FOUNDER**  
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Prospective partner:** University of Ghana Careers and Counselling Directorate (UGCCD)  
**Provider:** iPurpose LLC  
**Working program name:** Decision-to-Action Pilot

## Purpose
Explore whether iPurpose can extend—not replace—career-development support between UGCCD touchpoints by helping students organize choices, decide on a responsible next step, and follow through using practical systems and optional ethical AI guidance.

## Already-offered scope (preserved from Oct 30 correspondence)
- **Participants:** up to 40 voluntary University of Ghana students, **age 18+**
- **Duration:** eight weeks, exact dates to be confirmed with UGCCD
- **Pilot price to UG:** waived for this initial evaluation; any extra expenses to be reviewed and mutually agreed, not presumed
- **UGCCD role sought, if willing:** designate one liaison, help recruit an agreed eligible cohort, permit one short orientation, and join midpoint and closeout reviews
- **iPurpose role proposed:** manage a bounded guided experience, technical operations and participant-facing materials, support/issue processes, and an appropriately aggregated outcomes report

## Student experience (subject to institutional review)
1. Plain-language orientation and a voluntary participation decision
2. Non-diagnostic reflection on strengths/priorities and learning or career decision
3. Map one or two verified pathways, with human source checking
4. Create and retain a one-page Decision-to-Action Plan
5. Optional AI assistance on low-risk tasks, with clear limitations and human review
6. Complete or attempt one personally chosen next step
7. Exit reflection and private feedback

**Student deliverable: One-page Decision-to-Action Plan**
- **What I am deciding:** [participant's words]
- **My strengths/needs/constraints:** [participant's words, no health/counselling details]
- **Two plausible choices and verified information sources:** [choices / sources]
- **My current decision and why:** [student]
- **One action within 14 days:** [action, date, potential support]
- **What will remain my own judgment versus where AI may help:** [student]
- **My help/referral contact:** [UG-approved human route]

## Safeguards
Not a psychological diagnosis, licensed counselling service, psychometric certification, admissions decision or guaranteed pathway to employment. UGCCD retains authority over student support, referrals and local career information. Participation is voluntary; non-participation must not create academic penalties. No sensitive counselling data should be entered. Design adequate accessibility and human escalation. Where information is optional, participants can choose not to supply it. Students retain their plan subject to agreed retention/export constraints.

**Pre-launch hard gate:** University written authorization, mutually acceptable participation/processing documents, assessment of Ghana Act 843 obligations and any registration requirements, cross-border vendor/data processing arrangements, security review, anonymized reporting design, data minimization, and synthetic-data technical QA. No student recruitment or data processing before signoff.

## Pilot learning metrics (provisional, not efficacy claims)
- >=70% of enrolled consenting participants finish a usable self-authored Decision-to-Action Plan by week 8
- >=60% of enrolled participants self-report undertaking one predetermined action
- >=70% of exit-survey respondents describe the experience as useful (4 or 5 of 5), with survey response rate reported
- baseline/exit self-rated clarity comparison only for paired respondents, with missing responses reported
- onboarding, completion, W4/W8 retention, support requests, accessibility/incident logs, and service-delivery costs

All outcomes are descriptive, no control group or causal attribution presumed. Any externally released results or endorsements require separate institution review/permission. Aggregate report only and suppress small cells under an agreed threshold.

## Decisions needed before launch
- Who is the institution's authorized sponsor and point of contact?
- Which student segment and which actual support gap does UGCCD want to serve?
- What exact activities, local sources and referral channels does UGCCD approve?
- What consent, access, data-sharing, cross-border, privacy and security requirements apply?
- Which staff member handles local recruitment, counselling escalations and weekly operational liaison?
- What are acceptable dates and participant support windows?
- Who receives the private closeout report and who could authorize a paid continuation discussion?
