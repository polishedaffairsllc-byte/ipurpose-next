# iPurpose | Institution-Delivered Licensing Architecture
**November 17, 2026 — internal product specification; NOT currently a fully packaged or approved license.**
**Strategic owner:** Founder & CEO. Engineering, evaluator, privacy reviewer, and facilitators are roles to be appointed; not current staff commitments.

## Executive architecture
iPurpose LLC retains its expression, trademarks, hosted code and authored training materials. Approved organizations may purchase a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to **deliver approved iPurpose curriculum to their own participants** using their existing instructors and LMS/workshop infrastructure, subject to contract and training. The abstract methodology or "purpose" idea is not exclusively protectable by a contract against the whole world; protect specific content, brand use, source materials, licensed software and confidential know-how, as counsel advises.

**Position:** "An institution-delivered decision-to-action curriculum that helps participants make informed choices, build practical follow-through systems, and use AI responsibly." iPurpose is a non-clinical learning support program, not an AI therapy product, psychometric assessment, accredited credential, or guaranteed job-placement tool.

## Commercial model — INTERNAL TEST QUOTES ONLY
1. **Facilitator-ready cohort pilot license:** **$3,500** for one 8-week cohort of up to 40 adults at one site, delivered by licensee staff. Includes licensed facilitator guide, student workbook/templates, two facilitator orientation seats in one virtual train-the-trainer session, standard participant reflection tools, a pre/post original 5-item non-diagnostic check, and aggregated evaluation/report template. No iPurpose-led weekly facilitation or live case management.
2. **Annual campus / organization site license:** **$12,000/year** for up to 250 participants at one named site, up to four authorized facilitators, approved curriculum versions/quarterly material updates, one remote staff onboarding and standard email technical assistance with agreed limits. Additional sites, participants, support, and integrations by separately negotiated amendment; no sublicensing.
3. **Founder/iPurpose-supported 8-week pilot:** **$7,500** for up to 40 participants with scoped iPurpose delivery assistance, as separately proposed. Do not double bill license and facilitation.
4. **Existing UGCCD pilot:** **$0 pilot fee (already offered)** for up to 40 voluntary 18+ students over 8 weeks; don't replace or reinterpret the commitment without partner agreement.

These prices and limits are hypotheses, not market-validated. Founder approval, tested margins, legal form and demonstrated fulfillability are required before quotation. Institutions may have budget and procurement restrictions; no guarantee of acceptance. Taxes, cross-border costs, additional participant support, accessibility customization and reporting workloads could materially change economics.

## Program "box" to build before license sales
### A. Licensed IP and materials
- Instructor-ready 8-week course map aligned to Soul → Systems → AI™ with plain-language adult learning objectives and one action-plan outcome.
- 8 facilitator session scripts: objectives, timing, questions, exercises, support boundary, homework and debrief.
- 8 participant worksheets and a single cumulative **Decision-to-Action Plan** (accessible PDF/Docs/LMS-friendly versions; review institution accessibility standards).
- Original baseline and follow-up 5-item **self-report instrument**, not validated diagnosis or clinical scale.
- Local pathway research checklist: sources, publication dates, local staff verification, and error flagging.
- Responsible AI playbook: task selection, hallucination checks, privacy and disclosure; opt-out for AI use.
- Trainer orientation slides, two-hour self-paced or guided facilitator prep, knowledge checks and instructor FAQ.
- Administrator implementation checklist: cohort activation, consent/eligibility, support escalation, privacy, reporting and offboarding.
- Aggregate outcomes template (denominator, attrition, action attempts/completions, usefulness, missingness, staff hours).

### B. Eight-week lesson map (editable to buyer's context, no major code change)
- **Week 1 Soul — Orient:** decide what question deserves attention; introduce student agency, support/referral bounds; baseline.
- **Week 2 Soul — Understand:** identify strengths, constraints, priorities and possibilities without diagnosis.
- **Week 3 Soul-to-Systems — Investigate:** gather credible local information on two future options; verify sources with staff.
- **Week 4 Systems — Decide:** compare options and articulate one chosen direction; adviser available for support.
- **Week 5 Systems — Build:** convert direction into a 14-day action plan, constraints and accountability.
- **Week 6 AI — Support:** optional safe AI use for planning, drafts or organization; cite and verify outputs; preserve human decisions.
- **Week 7 Systems — Do:** attempt real next step; log whether completed, what prevented follow-through, and a safe adjustment.
- **Week 8 Soul/Systems/AI — Reflect:** update plan, choose next step, exit survey, secure student-owned export.

### C. Different licensed editions (reuse 80–90% content; don't build separate software)
- **Higher Education / Emerging Leaders:** first-year seminars, senior capstones, student success, career services.
- **Workforce / Career Transition:** employer re-entry, adult education, workforce coaching, appropriate career-resource referrals.
- **Entrepreneurship / Small Business:** cohort-to-action; decisions, workflows and practical AI; no business-income promises.

Local partners validate industry and employment resources. Each edition has different examples and referral boundaries, not different proprietary core frameworks.

## Technical delivery tiers and product gaps
**Stage 1 / zero-integration curriculum license (preferred):** institution distributes licensed instructor and participant assets in existing Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, Google Classroom or secure portal; collects data under its own university policies using approved survey templates and provides appropriately aggregated results. **Do not claim tested LMS packages or integrations** until QA on exact systems. No user login to iPurpose is required for curriculum-only version if the assessment content can legally and pedagogically be delivered as instructor-owned worksheets.

**Stage 2 / hosted add-on (future):** web/app Clarity and Compass access with institution-managed cohort IDs, privacy isolation, role-based aggregation, access limits, deletion, export and accessibility. No existing site-level admin or SSO is asserted. Price separately after security review and roadmap.

**Not yet packaged in the current codebase:** complete standalone 8-week facilitator curriculum; downloadable complete workbook; facilitator certification/assessment; ready LMS modules/SCORM/xAPI; institutional roster/seat provisioning; site-level licensing/expiry; instructor dashboards; secure cohort-level aggregate report/export; terms governing redistribution and partner IP; standard buyer invoicing/procurement. Engineer to verify each against code before statements of readiness.

## License agreement clauses for lawyer review, NOT legal advice
- Scope: one legal customer, named sites, dates/term, participants and facilitator seats, permitted modifications or no modifications, approved partner attribution ("iPurpose™-powered").
- License restrictions: no resale, sublicensing, redistribution, public posting of facilitator manual, model-training uploads, removal of brand attribution, derivative commercial products without permission.
- Participant rights: students own their submitted personal reflections/action plans; cannot appropriate student content; provider only accesses minimal licensed program data.
- Platform/data: separate controller/processor agreement where necessary, prohibited data categories, provider/subprocessor list, no sensitive counseling notes to third-party AI, retention/deletion/breach process, accessibility and educational privacy review.
- Staff training and service scope: program materials are educational, not counseling or medical care; local institution handles referrals/support.
- Fees/renewal/payment, cure period, updates, versioning, termination/export, IP infringement claims, liability limitations and applicable law.
- Quality: optional logo use only under brand guide, no promise of credential/certification unless actually created and validated.
- Avoid suggesting methodology concept alone has patent/exclusive protection; discuss copyright/trademark and actual licensing agreement with qualified IP attorney.

## Economics and distribution assumptions
Content licensing can reduce founder delivery hours, but annual content revisions, trainer onboarding, quality monitoring, support and sales remain ongoing costs. Pilot license $3,500 for 40 represents $87.50 per fully enrolled seat; annual $12,000 for 250 represents $48 at full use. Buyers are purchasing use rights and support—not data access, app source code or guaranteed outcomes. **The $7,500 managed pilot and $3,500 self-delivered pilot are alternatives**, not additive fees.

## Acceptance criteria and owners
- **Founder (A):** final package design, IP/brand use, pricing, pricing constraints, buyer agreements and sign-off.
- **ChatGPT during live work sessions (R, strategy):** draft curriculum framework, facilitator materials, model licensing scope, buyer outreach and scorecards.
- **Codex/engineering (R when assigned):** inventory existing assets, produce secure exports, ensure accessible formats, create versioned download bundles and license provisioning only when buyer need proven; QA distribution in 1–2 LMS systems, no production disruption.
- **Qualified privacy/IP counsel (R when retained):** licensing terms, privacy, student data, program exceptions, appropriate disclosures.
- **Institution program manager (R only after agreement):** select facilitator(s), recruit adult participants, deliver learning activities and offer support/referrals, report aggregates.
- **Independent evaluation lead (R when assigned):** measure meaningful outcomes without overstating causal impact.

**First execution gate:** build a **minimum licensable product (MLP)** in a document packet with Weeks 1–2 complete, outline Weeks 3–8, participant plan, original survey, and facilitator onboarding. Obtain one candid buyer review on usefulness and workload before completing rest of content. Never sell an unbuilt curriculum as "ready."
