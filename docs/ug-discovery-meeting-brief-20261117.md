## Meeting objective (30 minutes)
Determine mutual fit and what approval path exists. **Not** to secure immediate procurement, solicit student records, or push another sales presentation.

## Suggested agenda
- 0–5 minutes: appreciate UGCCD's existing career services; confirm their priority and capacity
- 5–15 minutes: listen for current gap (students between appointments, decision clarity, follow-through)
- 15–22 minutes: explain exact limited pilot, show synthetic student Decision-to-Action Plan
- 22–27 minutes: university privacy, safeguards, liaison, academic calendar, institutional authorization
- 27–30 minutes: summarize next action, decision owner and date, ask permission to send a revised one-page charter after the meeting

## Five questions to ask
1. Which career-development challenge creates the most difficulty between student appointments, and how do you currently address it?
2. What kinds of adult students would be suitable for a voluntary eight-week pilot? Are there exclusions or accessibility requirements?
3. Which personal information and AI uses would the University prohibit or require institutional controls for?
4. Who would oversee local recruitment, student-support referral, privacy approval and approval of Ghana-specific career information?
5. If a free pilot produces useful evidence, which office would evaluate a paid continuation and what would that process require?

## What to show
- Existing, approved UGCCD one-pager and eight-slide concept (no new unapproved deck)
- Synthetic/fictional student decision-to-action example; no student records

## What to listen for
- Problem urgency: does UGCCD recognize the precise gap?
- Institutional ownership: named sponsor and potential liaison
- Program feasibility: volunteer availability, workload, real career resources
- Safeguards: privacy, risk of inappropriate AI guidance, academic/counselling boundaries
- Future buyer: procurement and budget authority, not just an interested program champion
