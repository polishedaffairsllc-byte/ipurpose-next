## B. Operating rules
1. One prospective anchor partner, one scope, one primary outcome. No new cold outreach, paid campaigns, new tiers, or feature builds unrelated to pilot readiness during the initial gate period.
2. No student recruitment, identifiable participant import, AI processing of student content, or live pilot before UG authorization, mutual pilot charter, lawful-basis/privacy review, data-flow review, and technical QA.
3. UG counsellors remain the support authority. No automated diagnoses, mental-health triage claims, admissions decisions, eligibility scores, or personalized career guarantees. Participants may decline or withdraw without academic penalty.
4. Institutional reporting must be aggregated, minimum-cell threshold agreed before use, and exclude private answers. Do not represent a pilot with N<=40 as a causal impact study.
5. Preserve existing UG proposal and assets. The prior #104 website sales-page/entrepreneur copy branch is a separate draft: **hold for strategic review** before any production merge; it narrows messaging toward small-business owners and may conflict with the institutional positioning.
6. Use UK/US/UG professional plain language as appropriate; University of Ghana must approve local context and sources before publication.
7. No additional spend or outside professional engagement without founder approval. Fee waiver applies to partner price, not an assertion that iPurpose has no costs.
8. NACE concepts can inform competency alignment; do not reproduce copyrighted commercial assessment materials without permission.

## C. Defined program and deliverable
**Program name (working only):** iPurpose Decision-to-Action Pilot.
**Proposed student deliverable:** one-page Decision-to-Action Plan with (1) strengths/priorities identified in the student's own words, (2) 1–2 possible career or learning pathways, (3) sources locally verified with UGCCD, (4) a chosen priority, (5) a 14-day action step with responsible owner/time, (6) human support contact, and (7) optional AI use disclosure/guardrails. Do not store sensitive counselling content.

**Proposed eight-week sequence (institution can revise):**
- W0 before launch: faculty/UGCCD approval, access and accessibility checks, participant invitation and voluntary consent
- W1: orientation and non-diagnostic baseline: clarity, action readiness, support needs
- W2: reflection on strengths, interests, values and constraints
- W3: identify two locally grounded career/learning options and check sources
- W4: compare options and document one decision with a counsellor-support route
- W5: convert the decision into a practical plan, calendar milestone, and follow-up reminder
- W6: responsible AI support where optional; evidence checking; do not disclose private counselling details to AI
- W7: carry out an action and capture self-reported evidence; human escalation if needed
- W8: review, exit survey, and participant-owned plan export or download; aggregate institutional debrief

**Core journey:** invitation → participant info / voluntary consent → account or institution-approved access → baseline → Clarity / reflection → plan → action follow-up → exit. Whether the existing mobile app or web delivery supports this exactly is an engineering question, not a claim of shipped functionality. Prefer existing capabilities and minimal manual facilitation rather than creating a new platform.

## D. Measurement and transparent decision rules
Define the cohort as all consented participants receiving access; count no-shows and dropouts in denominator. Report actual numerator/denominator for every metric, by week.
- Primary measure: % of all enrolled participants who finish a usable Decision-to-Action Plan by W8; **provisional go threshold >=70%**.
- Follow-through: % of all enrolled participants who self-report undertaking at least one predefined action within the pilot; **provisional >=60%**. Do not call unverified self-report externally verified.
- Usefulness: % of exit-survey respondents rating the program useful (4 or 5 on 5-point scale); **provisional >=70%**, alongside response rate.
- Participation: start rate, weekly completion, W4 and W8 retention, time to first plan.
- Descriptive change: baseline-to-exit clarity/agency ratings among paired respondents; report distribution and missingness. No efficacy or causal claims.
- Institutional fit: sponsor asks to review results and considers a paid follow-on / procurement discussion (not equivalent to purchase).
- Safety gate: **zero unresolved serious privacy/safeguarding incidents** and required deletion/export checks pass.
- Commercial go additionally requires credible procurement owner, pricing path, estimated delivery cost and achievable margin; a positive free pilot alone is not proof of paid demand.

**Decision outcomes:** GO to a narrowly scoped paid pilot conversation if outcomes and safeguarding gates pass; ADAPT if engagement is high but action weak; STOP/REDESIGN if controls fail, harm occurs, or engagement/utility is consistently poor. Provisional thresholds are management choices for review with UG, not external scientific standards.
