## Decision summary — recommended, subject to founder confirmation
1. **Soul:** Retain canonical **Soul → Systems → AI™**. Recommended first-use gloss: **Soul = values, purpose, and self-understanding** (“what matters to you and why”). Systems = practical steps and support. AI = responsible tools that assist, but never replace, human judgment. “Purpose and judgment” (v1 pp.1–2) is acceptable, but leaves the personal reflection aspect less clear and repeats “judgment” awkwardly; use the improved explanation in institutional copy, without claiming clinical or religious meaning.
2. **Typography:** Italiana for display; Marcellus for short headings. Recommend **Source Sans 3** for workbook body and instructions as an accessible companion font, **subject to Founder/Style Bible review** of the previously two-font rule. Use at least a comfortable 12pt print body target and accessible digital scale, not only fixed sizes; meet contrast, spacing and reflow expectations. Do not bundle/distribute font files.
3. **International prices:** Keep **$3,500 cohort / $12,000 annual / $7,500 managed** as **internal unvalidated USD hypotheses**. No automatic FX conversion or globally advertised rate card. International scope, local buying power, lawful contract/payment/tax issues and service support cost must be reviewed case-by-case. **Ghana eight-week fee waiver is binding as an existing communicated proposal assumption; do not amend unilaterally.**
4. **Facilitator:** Public-facing interim term **“institution-designated facilitator”** rather than “qualified facilitator,” unless requirements formally documented. Working standard: a university/workforce/entrepreneurship program educator, advisor or trainer **authorized by the institution**, who completes iPurpose-specific orientation on curriculum, person-led decisions, privacy, responsible AI, role boundaries and referral protocols; able to conduct accessible group facilitation and follow institution policies. **Not** “iPurpose certified,” not a regulated clinical credential, not a guarantee of competence. The institution retains any required safeguarding/counselor expertise.

## Claims verification — source-backed research, not efficacy
**Verified Nov 17, 2026 from source pages:**
- Strada Education Foundation, **Oct 13 2026, Quality Coaching and Guidance**, https://www.strada.org/reports/quality-coaching-and-guidance-experiences-sources-and-student-confidence : In its **survey** of seniors at public four-year universities and non-dual-enrollment public associate-degree students, only **3%** and **7%**, respectively, reported **all three** elements of quality coaching. Those elements: personalized guidance, timely labor-market information, and goal/obstacle support. This does **not** mean 97%/93% lacked all support or were unhappy/purposeless; many received one or two components. Say “comprehensive combination of elements,” and specify studied populations.
- NACE, **Oct 12 2026, first national benchmarks of career readiness**, https://www.naceweb.org/career-readiness/trends-and-predictions/the-state-of-career-readiness-the-first-national-benchmarks-study-of-americas-college-students : over **17,000 students** assessed; career and self-development emerged as the area with greatest need for help, notably skills identifying and securing professional-development opportunities. NACE does not validate iPurpose, establish demand for paid licenses, or establish career-outcome improvements.

**Safe research-supported selling sentence:** “Recent research documents gaps in comprehensive education-to-career guidance and career self-development skills. iPurpose is developing a facilitator-delivered program to test whether structured reflection, choice, action planning and follow-through can complement existing support.”

**Do not say:** “iPurpose improves retention/employment/placement/agency”; “universities aren't serving students”; “students are generally unhappy with degrees”; “every participant finishes a plan”; “data will remain private” as a blanket guarantee before a reviewed data flow.
