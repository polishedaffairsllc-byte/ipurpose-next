# Business Plan

This is a template for a concise, actionable business plan. Fill in each section with your specifics.

---

## 1. Executive Summary
- Business name: 
- Founder(s): 
- Mission statement: (one sentence)
- Vision: (one paragraph)
- What we do / value proposition: (one paragraph)
- Stage: (idea / validated / revenue / scaling)

---

## 2. Problem & Opportunity
- Problem statement: who has the problem and why it matters
- Current alternatives / competition
- Market size (TAM / SAM / SOM) — rough estimates

---

## 3. Solution & Product
- Product or service description
- Key features and benefits
- Why it's different / unfair advantage

---

## 4. Target Market & Customers
- Primary customer segments
- Ideal customer profile (ICP)
- Customer personas
- Channels to reach customers

---

## 5. Business Model
- How you make money (pricing, revenue streams)
- Unit economics (price, cost to serve, contribution margin)
- Sales cycle and customer lifetime value (LTV)

---

## 6. Go-to-Market Strategy
- Marketing channels (content, partnerships, paid ads, organic)
- Sales strategy and ops
- Key metrics & KPIs to track (CAC, conversion, retention)

---

## 7. Competition & Positioning
- Competitor landscape (direct, indirect)
- Differentiation and positioning statement

---

## 8. Operations & Team
- Core team and roles
- Key hires needed
- Operational processes (production, fulfillment, support)

---

## 9. Financial Plan
- 12–24 month forecast (revenue, expenses, net)
- Milestones and funding needs
- Assumptions (conversion rates, pricing, growth rates)

---

## 10. Risks & Mitigations
- Top risks (market, product, execution, legal) and how to mitigate

---

## 11. Milestones & Roadmap
- 30 / 90 / 180 day goals
- 1 year goals
- 3 year vision

---

## 12. Appendix
- Relevant docs: pitch deck, customer interviews, financial model, legal
