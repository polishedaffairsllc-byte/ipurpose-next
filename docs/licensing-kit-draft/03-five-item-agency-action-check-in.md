# iPurpose | Original Five-Item Decision & Action Check-In (DRAFT)
**Not psychometrically validated.** For adult learner program improvement only; not a screening, diagnosis, admission, hiring, counseling, or intervention eligibility determination. Any aggregate sharing must be authorized and privacy reviewed.

## Instructions
Answer based on how you feel **right now**, not how you think others expect you to answer.

Rating: 1 = Strongly disagree; 2 = Disagree; 3 = Not sure; 4 = Agree; 5 = Strongly agree; prefer not to answer is always acceptable.

1. I can describe one important academic, career or business decision I want to make.
2. I know where to find trustworthy information that could help me compare my options.
3. I can name what matters most to me when choosing between realistic options.
4. I can identify one specific, manageable action to take within the next 14 days.
5. I know at least one appropriate person or service I could contact if I need support.

**Optional free-response prompt:** "What is one concrete step you want to take in the next two weeks?" No sensitive data requested.

## When to administer
- At Week 1 onboarding, before instruction, if the program has approved measurement.
- At Week 8 closeout or sooner if a shorter workshop/preview is being evaluated.
- Report how many eligible participants responded to both surveys, separately from total enrolled cohort and each item; allow skip without penalty.

## Recommended aggregate-only report
- Invitations and consenting adult cohort (denominator), completed baseline N, completed exit N, matched paired N if ethically linkable and approved.
- Item medians/distribution at each time; paired changes only among matched respondents, missingness disclosed.
- Action-plan completion count and self-reported action attempted/completed count against all enrolled participants.
- Participant usefulness survey respondent count and % reporting useful; no causal inference, no claims that life "purpose" has been scientifically measured.
- Suppress small groups and never expose private narratives.

## What not to do
- Do not classify students as "misaligned", "without purpose", "low agency" or "at risk" based on a 5-question score.
- Do not imply a statistically significant effect from a small uncontrolled pilot.
- Do not share identifiable scores with institutional managers or funders without approved purpose, role, access and legal basis.
- Do not compare outcomes across institutions without harmonized surveys, consent/retention protocols and an appropriate analytic plan.
