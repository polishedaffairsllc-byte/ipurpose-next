**Product unity:** same participant-owned Decision-to-Action Plan, reflection, systems and optional ethical AI supports. Institution-specific examples and guardrails can vary. The *buyer* is program sponsor/operator; the *end user* is a participant; the *champion* is a coach/program director; the *economic buyer* is budget owner, often not the champion. Identify each explicitly.

### Where the market already works
- **Adult workforce:** career coaches and case managers use assessments and plans, so iPurpose should **not** claim to replace assessments, counsellors, or proven workforce programs. Test a narrow wedge: help a participant execute one next action **between coaching sessions**, while optionally producing a low-burden aggregate program report.
- **Higher education:** after student reflection/career appointments, guide a 14-day action follow-through.
- **Entrepreneurship programs:** between workshops, help founders pick a priority, establish one workflow and use AI responsibly. This should not be confused with a general consumer AI tutorial.

## Outreach process — controlled, not volume-based
**Stage 0 — Verification** (ChatGPT/research R; Founder A): Ensure official public program evidence and exact business email; search prior correspondence. Segregate prospect list from UG and prior university proposals.
**Stage 1 — Founder-reviewed first contact** (ChatGPT draft R; Founder A/sender): Send 2 workforce emails first (ACSS and ULGA Pillar II); send MAX network intro separately. Do not send TEC entrepreneurship draft until program positioning is approved. Avoid links to unfinished institutional product pages or unapproved pilot charter.
**Stage 2 — Response** (Founder R; ChatGPT supports): Reply with a two-sentence description and two options for 15–20 minute calls. No invented meeting date.
**Stage 3 — Qualifying discovery** (Founder R, Strategy supports): Ask what current staff can already do; what gap remains between touchpoints; specific cohort and staffing; what outcomes funders require; who holds spending authority; whether a pilot would be considered and what approval gates apply. If no recognized gap, do not pitch harder.
**Stage 4 — Written synthesis** (ChatGPT/Evaluation R): Score need, urgency, appropriate program owner, procurement route, privacy burden, cost, and capacity. Present pilot only if at least one program operator says a real gap exists.
**Stage 5 — Pilot proposal** (Founder A; Strategy drafts): 4–6 week workforce **option** subject to fit, privacy review and mutually accepted costs. Do not auto-waive a fee because UG's waiver was a separate offer. Adapt participants/examples only, reuse same core product. Minimum one measurable participant action; no guarantee.
**Stage 6 — Conversion** (Founder A): Determine sponsor vs. payer; scope, who signs, costs, permitted funding, required contract/data controls. Do not classify meeting or interest as revenue.

### Roles and work boundaries
- **Founder/CEO:** Accountable for positioning, authorizing and sending external messages, conducting 15–20 minute discovery calls, financial commitments, and partner agreements; no self-assignment of all back-office tasks.
- **ChatGPT in active sessions:** Research, draft tailored emails, build contact qualification notes, summarize responses, maintain decision grid; no autonomous background outreach.
- **Codex/engineer:** Only once a qualified program requirement is identified: map it onto existing product capabilities, estimate gap/effort, implement after review. No duplicate workforce app.
- **Program/evaluation lead:** Define action-plan and aggregate outcome measures; privacy reviewer considers participant data and vulnerable client populations; never request case-management/clinical data from a first-contact email.
- **Prospective institution/partner:** Not assigned any work, resources or sponsorship until confirmed in writing.

### Decision rules for who needs iPurpose
A target qualifies only if (a) staff can articulate a specific **between-touchpoints follow-through** problem; (b) participants voluntarily can access a safe experience; (c) action-plan completion or action follow-through are relevant outcomes; (d) a budget holder or sponsor is identifiable; (e) pilot governance/privacy is feasible. The presence of similar services on a website proves **mission adjacency**, not a need for iPurpose.

### Competitive caution
Georgia has already launched career-navigation initiatives and workforce providers generally have coaching and action-planning tools. Sell neither generic "AI" nor a duplicate career assessment. Demonstrate a distinctive, measurable continuation layer. Compare alternative solutions and do not claim higher efficacy without evidence.
