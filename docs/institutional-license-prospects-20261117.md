## Outreach and discovery discipline
- **Don't send 20 emails simultaneously.** First review the 3 P0 drafts (ACSS, Urban League workforce, MAX). Keep entrepreneurship draft as reserve. Then send a small, well-researched tranche of three additional organizations: ACE WBC, Start:ME, BRIDGE or Greater Wealth Works once licensed materials and current opportunities are understood.
- **No new university emails until scheduled queue is reconciled**. The existing university sequence should continue as requested; it is not modified by this work.
- **Open with a question about unmet between-session execution needs**, not an assertion that participants are out of purpose or that iPurpose has proven impact.
- **Identify 3 people/roles:** champion (program manager), economic buyer (budget/contract owner), delivery owner (trainer/facilitator). They may differ.
- **Qualification:** real unsolved problem, current intervention and alternatives, frequency/severity, acceptable participant group, budget authority, delivery feasibility, privacy/accessibility risk.
- **Evidence:** "Already sent / opened / interested" must come from actual correspondence or reliable first-party events, not assumptions or page traffic.
- **Responsible work:** only public professional contact details; honor opt-out; no bulk unsolicited blasts, student lists, or unemployment claimant records.
- **Founder:** approves direct contact and license pricing. **ChatGPT:** research/drafts/response analysis during active sessions. **Engineering:** package minimum licensable product and verify performance; **not** independent mass outreach.

## License language for prospect conversations
**Not yet for sale; exploratory:** "We're developing a facilitator-ready version of iPurpose that could let an organization license a structured eight-week decision-to-action curriculum for use in its own existing programs. I'm exploring whether your participants need additional support taking action between training or coaching sessions, and whether a self-delivered program would be useful."

Do not falsely claim downloadable licensed content, validated assessment, trained facilitators, platform integration, or approved institutional customers before verified.
