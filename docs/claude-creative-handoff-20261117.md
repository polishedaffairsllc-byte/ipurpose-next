**Prepared:** November 17, 2026

## 1. Executive handoff — where the business has landed
iPurpose LLC is shifting its **primary commercial hypothesis** from high-frequency social media driving individual $27/$47 self-service purchases to **institutional program licensing**. The company is **not** abandoning individual customers, public content, web/mobile apps, or its mission. Rather, those are supporting channels and optional tools; the primary near-term sales effort should target universities, workforce development organizations, career-transition providers, and later entrepreneurship cohorts.

**Core proposition:** iPurpose is a *human-centered decision-to-action methodology* helping participants reflect on what matters, make informed choices, establish practical follow-through systems, and use AI responsibly **without surrendering agency**.

**Canonical framework:** Soul → Systems → AI™. Preserve: “Alignment before action. Structure before scale. Automation after clarity.”

**Not the proposition:** generic AI coaching, another productivity app, diagnosing people as purposeless, replacing career advising/counselling, guaranteed job outcomes, or a new independent institutional app.

### Market and people
- **A. Higher education (live anchor conversation):** early students, capstone/senior cohorts, emerging leaders, and learners navigating academic-to-work transitions. Institutional buyer/champion: career services, student success, first-year programs or continuing education. University of Ghana Careers and Counselling Directorate expressed willingness to explore; **no partnership, meeting or participant cohort has been authorized**. On October 30 the founder already offered an **8-week pilot, up to 40 voluntary students aged 18+, with a fee waiver**. The Directorate requested early-December discussion.
- **B. Workforce development (second active acquisition lane):** adults affected by layoff or career changes and people navigating training or job transitions. Buyers: nonprofit workforce program directors and budget owners, WorkSource-related operators, sponsoring organizations. Four tailored **unsent** Gmail drafts were made to workforce/entrepreneurship/network contacts; do not duplicate them or send them.
- **C. Entrepreneurship support (reserve lane):** incubators, WBCs, small-business training cohorts; organizations sponsor participant access, not direct-to-consumer conversion as the first goal.

**Important two-level message:**
- **For participants:** “Find clarity about your next step, then build the structure and confidence to act.”
- **For institutions:** “Add a repeatable, facilitator-delivered process that helps participants turn reflection and career/learning choices into documented action and follow-through.”

This dual-language distinction is central. Claude's prior analysis usefully identified a person at a life/career turning point and the emotional-language signal. Keep those insights without making women 40–60 the exclusive audience or re-centering YouTube Shorts as the main growth engine.

## 2. Commercial model: internal test prices, not approved external quotes
Institution-delivered **8-week cohort pilot license**: **$3,500** for one site/cohort of up to 40 adult participants; institution's facilitators teach from a licensed guide and participant workbook after onboarding. An **annual site license** is hypothesized at **$12,000** for up to 250 participants, one site, up to four facilitators and defined updates/support. A distinct **iPurpose-supported pilot** is proposed at **$7,500**, including scoped iPurpose delivery assistance. A single virtual **90-minute workshop** was also considered at **$1,500**. These price/seat/support numbers are unvalidated planning hypotheses, not an approved or fully deliverable product.

**Exception:** University of Ghana's already-offered pilot fee is **waived ($0)**; no retrospective charge or replacement offer. Never imply Ghana has accepted the proposal or authorized pilot activities.

**Delivery vision:** institution buys a bounded, non-exclusive right to use *specific authored curriculum and materials*, delivered by its own personnel within its existing LMS/classrooms/workforce programs. iPurpose provides educational IP, instructor material, participant templates, training, content quality rules, and measurement framework. Student private reflections remain controlled by appropriate owners and institution policy. A hosted app or LMS integration is a **future optional add-on**, not a feature to promise today.

## 3. Current maturity, risks and evidence
Existing: framework, web/app Clarity/Compass concepts, university concept materials, outreach, preliminary charter and 8-week curricular map. **Not yet ready**: complete eight-session facilitator guide, student workbook, trainer kit, accessible LMS-ready files, actual institutional licensing agreement, site licensing/seat provisioning, secure cohort dashboard and validated outcomes.

Pilot measures proposed, **not a proven intervention**: >=70% of enrolled participants create usable Decision-to-Action Plans; >=60% self-report at least one defined action; >=70% of exit-survey respondents find experience useful, with response rates and dropouts disclosed. These are planning thresholds, not scientific benchmarks or observed results.

External studies of career guidance/readiness (Strada, NACE, NSSE) suggest a **guidance/action support problem**, not the efficacy of iPurpose. Do not imply degrees cause unhappiness or students are universally “out of purpose.” No fabricated testimonials or institutional endorsements. No clinical, psychometric, credential, career-placement, grant-eligibility or guaranteed ROI claims.

Existing university email schedule should continue unchanged; no new duplicate university contact campaign. Personal social content may continue at a sustainable low cadence for credibility but is not the central paid funnel. Draft PR #104 is unmerged and contains entrepreneur-specific homepage language; it must not be deployed before positioning is reviewed.

## 5. Brand and accessibility guardrails
- Preserve iPurpose and **Soul → Systems → AI™**. Tagline “Alignment before action. Structure before scale. Automation after clarity.”
- Established typography: **Italiana** for appropriate display usage and **Marcellus** for readable editorial emphasis; use accessible body copy and avoid oversized decorative paragraphs. Do not distribute font files.
- Core color character: **Deep Indigo, Champagne, Salmon Peach, Mist**. Use brand tokens/style references if available; do not invent an entirely new palette.
- Style: sophisticated, warm, human-first, clean and credible for institutional buyers—not mystical, motivational overload, generic tech gradients, or overproduced stock imagery.
- Use plain language a nontechnical reader can follow, appropriate contrast, alt-text prompts, and accessible structured content. Treat editable source as primary asset.
- No natal charts, personality diagnosis, unverifiable archetype science or speculative demographic claims. Avoid stigma or “your life is broken” messaging.
- Label everything **Draft / For Review**, no fictional contracts, ROI, accreditation, partnerships, testimonies or consent approval.
